# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

OpportunityBox exists to make opportunities reachable for people who don't have the right connections. That goal only works if the project itself is welcoming. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, nationality, level of experience, disability, appearance, gender identity, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and first-time contributors; everyone starts somewhere.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people the project serves.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately posting misleading opportunities, spam, or promotional content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

Report problems to **s.m.asif.hossain.1204@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed as quickly as possible.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.
